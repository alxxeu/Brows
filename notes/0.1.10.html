What's new

holoholo for macOS · Version 0.1.10 · November 11, 2026

New

  • Private Space. A locked space for private browsing, opened with Touch ID or your password. Open it with ⇧⌘N, by swiping past your last Space, from the Spaces menu, or by right-clicking the bottom bar. Its tabs come back after you restart holoholo, stored encrypted on your Mac. Cookies and site data are kept only until you quit holoholo. Nothing from it goes to the archive, search history or the downloads list, and extensions don't run in it. The space locks again when you leave it or lock your screen.
  • Tweaks. Change how a site looks, for every page of that site: hide elements you don't want (⌥⌘Z), pick a font or tone, narrow the page, or add your own CSS. Open the panel with ⌥⌘B.
  • Companions. Let tools like Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor look at your tabs and act in them. It's off by default; turn it on in Settings → Companions. Each companion asks first and you choose which Spaces it can reach and whether it can only read or also act.

Improved

  • A hidden sidebar now stays on screen over an empty page.
